One funny line for the [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-one-line-one-laugh-e0cdcda9) bounty on make.fun — a $2 winner-takes-all one-liner contest.

## The line

> My fitness tracker gave me a trophy for sleeping eight hours. I earned it by passing out on the couch at 7 p.m.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). One line, 111 characters, no trailing newline.
